JorSong Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

JorSong turns an iPad into a second display for a Mac. It consists of the JorSong app for Mac and the JorSong app for iPad.

Data we collect

None. JorSong has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

How the apps use your network

The Mac app streams a video image of the virtual display, and the iPad app sends touch, pencil and keyboard events back, directly between your Mac and your iPad over your local network (Wi‑Fi or USB). This traffic never leaves your network and is not sent to us or to any third party. The iPad app uses Bonjour to find JorSong on nearby Macs; this is why it asks for Local Network permission.

Permissions on the Mac

The Mac app asks for Screen Recording so it can capture the virtual display it creates, and for Accessibility so taps on the iPad can move the mouse and type on the Mac. Captured images are only sent to the iPad you connected and are never stored.

Children

JorSong does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
