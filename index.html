JorSong

Turn your iPad into a second display for your Mac over Wi‑Fi or USB. Hardware H.264 streaming, Apple Pencil, trackpad and keyboard support. No account, no analytics.

Download JorSong for Mac (.dmg) Source on GitHub

How to use

  1. On the Mac (macOS 14 or later): open the .dmg, drag JorSong to Applications, launch it and allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when asked.
  2. On the iPad: install JorSong from the App Store, open it and tap your Mac under "Macs on this network" (or type the Mac's address).
  3. Arrange the new display in System Settings → Displays like any monitor.

No iPad app? Open http://<your-mac>:8787 in Safari for the JPEG web client.
